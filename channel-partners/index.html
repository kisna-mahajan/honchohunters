What is the Honcho Hunters Channel Partner Network?

The Honcho Hunters Channel Partner Network enables independent recruiters, recruitment consultants and talent acquisition professionals in India to collaborate on recruitment opportunities through two models: a success-sharing partnership linked to successful placements, and an approved-profile partnership linked to candidate profile approval under agreed terms. Each model has its own process and commercial conditions.

Two Partnership Options

Choose Your Partnership Model

Choose the model that best suits your professional experience, recruitment capabilities and preferred way of working.

Option 1

Success-Sharing Partnership

Contribute to successful placements. Share in the success.

This model is designed for experienced recruitment professionals who want to collaborate with Honcho Hunters on recruitment assignments and participate in the agreed commercial reward when a successful placement is achieved.

You identify and refer suitable candidates, apply your recruitment expertise and work within the agreed assignment framework. Your share of the recruitment fee is determined by the commercial terms agreed for the relevant assignment.

This model may be right for you if you:

  • Have experience in recruitment, executive search or talent acquisition.
  • Maintain a strong professional network within specific industries or functions.
  • Prefer assignment-based collaboration linked to successful placements.
  • Want to develop a long-term professional relationship with Honcho Hunters.
  • Value quality, confidentiality and professional credibility.

Explore the complete model, working process, partnership levels and FAQs.

Option 2

Approved-Profile Partnership

Identify suitable candidates. Earn against approved profiles.

This model is designed for recruitment professionals who prefer a profile-based arrangement in which payment is linked to candidate profiles approved under the applicable terms.

The focus is on identifying relevant, carefully screened candidates who meet the requirements of specific assignments communicated by Honcho Hunters.

This model may be right for you if you:

  • Have access to relevant candidate networks, professional contacts or talent databases.
  • Understand specific industries, functions and recruitment requirements.
  • Prefer a defined profile-submission and evaluation process.
  • Can consistently identify suitable candidates rather than submit CVs in bulk.
  • Value transparent evaluation criteria and clearly communicated commercial terms.

The profile approval criteria, payment arrangements and working process are explained on the dedicated page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Channel Partner FAQs

What are the two Channel Partner models?

Option 1 is the Success-Sharing Partnership, where the partner participates in the agreed recruitment fee when a successful placement occurs under the applicable terms.

Option 2 is the Approved-Profile Partnership, where payment is linked to the approval of submitted candidate profiles under the applicable terms.

Which option should I choose?

Choose Option 1 if you prefer placement-linked success sharing. Choose Option 2 if you prefer a profile-based arrangement with payment linked to approved profiles.

Review both dedicated pages before making your decision.

Can I apply for both options?

You may review both models. Participation in one or both will depend on Honcho Hunters' approval and the applicable terms.

Is prior recruitment experience necessary?

Relevant recruitment experience or demonstrated candidate-sourcing capability is important. Applicants are assessed on their professional background, industry knowledge and suitability for the selected model.

Are assignments or earnings guaranteed?

No. Assignments, approved profiles, successful placements and earnings depend on business requirements, candidate suitability, evaluation criteria and the applicable agreement.

How does payment work?

The payment mechanism depends on the selected model. Success-sharing payments follow the agreed placement and fee-sharing terms, while approved-profile payments follow the applicable profile approval and payment conditions.

Please refer to the relevant option page for details.

Can I work independently or part-time?

The partnership is intended for independent professional collaboration, subject to the applicable terms. Your ability to participate will depend on your availability, assignment requirements and the selected model.

How are partnership levels awarded?

Where partnership levels apply, progression is based on sustained performance, quality, professionalism, confidentiality and business requirements. Higher status does not guarantee assignments or earnings.

How do I apply?

Visit your preferred option page and follow its application instructions: Option 1 - Success-Sharing Partnership or Option 2 - Approved-Profile Partnership.
