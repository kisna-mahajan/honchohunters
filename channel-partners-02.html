The Process

How the Partnership Works

  1. Step 1 - Apply and Get Onboarded

    Share your recruitment experience, industry expertise and candidate-sourcing capabilities. Suitable applicants may be invited for an initial discussion.

  2. Step 2 - Receive an Assignment

    Honcho Hunters communicates relevant recruitment requirements, essential qualifications, experience criteria, submission instructions and applicable payout information.

  3. Step 3 - Shortlist 1–3 Suitable Candidates

    Review your candidate network and identify the strongest matches for the specific assignment. Screen candidates before submission rather than forwarding resumes in bulk.

  4. Step 4 - Submit Profiles for Evaluation

    Submit the shortlisted resumes in the prescribed format. Candidate contact details remain masked during initial evaluation, in accordance with the submission process.

  5. Step 5 - Profile Evaluation and Approval

    The Honcho Hunters team evaluates each resume against the assignment requirements, considering domain relevance, functional suitability, stability, quality of experience and overall suitability.

  6. Step 6 - Receive Your Payout

    For every resume formally approved, you earn ₹500–₹2,000, depending on the role and seniority.

    Payment is processed within 72 hours of approval.

    The approved-profile payout is not dependent on the candidate ultimately being placed, subject to the applicable programme terms.

Evaluation Criteria

What Makes a Resume Approval-Worthy?

We value thoughtful screening and professional judgement. Every submission may be evaluated on the following parameters:

Domain Relevance

Does the candidate have the required industry experience?

Functional Suitability

Does the candidate's expertise match the position's responsibilities?

Quality of Experience

Has the candidate handled relevant assignments, projects, teams or business outcomes?

Seniority

Does the candidate's professional level match the position?

Career Stability

Is the candidate's employment history appropriate for the assignment?

Accuracy

Is the information supplied current, consistent and materially accurate?

Overall Suitability

Does the candidate meet the assignment's essential requirements?

The specific criteria may vary by assignment.

Our Submission Rule

Submit only 1–3 carefully shortlisted resumes against each assignment. Avoid:

  • Bulk resume submissions
  • Duplicate candidate profiles
  • Generic resumes unrelated to the requirement
  • Unscreened or outdated profiles
  • Profiles submitted without the candidate's consent

Our principle: quality over quantity, every time.

Earnings

Earnings and Payment Terms

How much will I earn?

You will earn ₹500–₹2,000 per approved resume, depending on the role, candidate seniority and assignment requirements. The applicable payout will be communicated for the assignment.

When will I be paid?

Payment is processed within 72 hours of formal resume approval.

Do I have to wait for a placement?

No. The payout under this partnership is linked to formal resume approval, not the candidate's eventual placement.

Is every submission paid?

No. Only resumes that meet the applicable assignment criteria and receive formal approval qualify for payment.

Does submitting more resumes increase my chances?

Not necessarily. Honcho Hunters encourages only 1–3 carefully shortlisted resumes per assignment. Bulk, duplicate and generic submissions are discouraged.

Can I earn regularly?

Your earnings depend on the availability of assignments, the suitability of candidates you identify and the number of profiles approved. Consistent quality may support further engagement, but a minimum assignment volume or income is not guaranteed.

Eligibility

Who Can Become a Channel Partner?

We welcome eligible professionals with recruitment experience, industry knowledge or a relevant candidate network. Suitable applicants include:

  • Independent Recruitment Consultants
  • Former Recruitment Agency Professionals
  • Executive-Search Researchers
  • Freelance Recruiters & Talent Sourcers
  • HR & Talent Acquisition Professionals
  • Retired or Semi-Retired Recruitment Professionals

The network is open to eligible recruitment professionals across India.

Who Should Not Apply?

This programme is not intended for:

  • Mass resume suppliers
  • Individuals expecting payment for every submission
  • People unwilling to screen candidates carefully
  • Individuals who submit duplicate or misleading profiles
  • Professionals unable to maintain confidentiality and candidate consent

We seek reliable partners who value quality, integrity and professional relationships.

Grow With Us

Partnership Levels

Honcho Hunters may recognise channel partners at different levels based on quality, reliability, responsiveness and professional conduct.

Level 1

Associate Channel Partner

For eligible professionals beginning their association with Honcho Hunters. The focus is on understanding assignment requirements, following submission procedures and establishing consistent sourcing quality.

Level 2

Channel Partner

For professionals who demonstrate reliable delivery, strong screening and relevant industry knowledge. Consistent performance may lead to consideration for additional assignments.

Level 3

Premier Channel Partner

The highest recognition within the network, reserved for professionals who demonstrate sustained quality, confidentiality, professionalism and commitment. Depending on business requirements, Premier Partners may be considered for priority assignment opportunities and a closer working association.

Partnership levels are determined by Honcho Hunters. They do not guarantee a minimum number of assignments or income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Approved-Profile Partnership FAQs

What is the Approved-Profile Channel Partner Programme?

It is a performance-based sourcing partnership through which recruitment professionals submit relevant candidate resumes against Honcho Hunters assignments and receive a payout for every profile formally approved.

Must I obtain candidate consent?

Yes. You must obtain the candidate's permission before sharing their resume and personal information with Honcho Hunters.

Can I work part-time?

Yes. The arrangement can suit independent and part-time recruitment professionals, subject to assignment requirements and deadlines.

Can I move to the Success-Sharing Partnership?

You may express interest in the alternative model. It operates under separate commercial terms and requires a separate agreement.
